I’ve been quietly building Agctor since May 2025. It’s time to share more of it.

The idea is simple: agents are actors. Each owns its state and communicates through messages. Agctor brings that approach to an open-source .NET framework for agentic systems.

Now I’m putting it to work in Cynelo, my AI video studio: coordinating shot planning, tracking characters and objects through Scene Memory, and handing rendering work to Cynelo—with human input when the workflow needs it.

That’s the part I’m excited about: taking an architecture I’ve been building quietly and using it to tackle real creative workflows.

Still building. Now sharing.
